# Pre-Registered Candidate Scoring Rubric — Track 2
*Committed BEFORE any candidate results are generated (pre-registration; commit date = registration date).
This rubric will not change after evidence assembly begins. If it must, the change + reason gets its own commit.*

Each candidate drug scores 0–2 on each criterion; weighted sum ranks candidates.

| # | Criterion | Weight | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mechanistic directionality | 3 | wrong/unknown sign vs variant effect (LoF vs GoF) | plausible sign, indirect node | correct sign at a node with direct evidence in the disease pathway |
| 2 | Human genetic / model support | 2 | none | model organism or cell rescue only | human interventional or genetic evidence in this or sibling pathway |
| 3 | Approval status | 2 | not approved anywhere | approved (any indication, any region) | approved with relevant route/formulation for a child |
| 4 | Pediatric dosing precedent | 2 | none | published pediatric use, other indication | established pediatric dosing guidance |
| 5 | Chronic-use safety in this patient context | 2 | immunosuppressive/genotoxic or CIN-aggravating | tolerable with monitoring | benign profile for chronic/intermittent use |
| 6 | Measurable PD biomarker available | 1 | none | indirect only | direct target-engagement or micronucleus-responsive readout |
| 7 | Falsifiability (a defined kill experiment) | 1 | none stated | in-vitro only | patient-cell or clinical-lab test specified |

Max = 26 (weighted). Candidates below 13 are reported only in the rejected/deprioritized table.
Hard vetoes regardless of score: (a) mechanism increases chromosome missegregation;
(b) efficacy depends on an intact SAC; (c) aneuploidy-selective lethality as chronic systemic therapy.

Negative controls to be run and reported: scrambled seed-gene set; verify an Mps1 inhibitor
surfaces in evidence assembly AND is caught by veto (a)/(b); leave-one-out on ATM/A-T must
recover NAD+ precursors.
